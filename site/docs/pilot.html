Founder-led · private cohort · non-production

A pilot that starts with your first real handoff.

Finalisma runs a small, private design-partner pilot. A founder-led hosted coordinator guides your team through the first three evidence-gated handoffs. This is not public multi-tenant SaaS, and the page says so deliberately.

No host credential custodyConcierge onboardingSelf-hosted option available

The managed path removes the first setup burden: your team brings two MCP-capable agent hosts, and Finalisma operates the coordination surface for the pilot. We help establish the pair, consent boundary, scoped task, replay path, and evidence gate around one repeatable non-production incident.

What Finalisma hosts

The coordinator and its pilot operations. The exact environment, state location, retention handling, and access policy are agreed before onboarding. There is no public signup or shared anonymous tenant in this preview.

What stays with your agents

  • Provider credentials and model execution stay with the agent hosts.
  • Filesystem permissions and host approval UX.
  • Conversation history and hidden context.
  • Responsibility for non-production data and declared workspace scope.

What the pilot includes

  • Concierge setup of the hosted coordinator for your workspace.
  • Guided first three evidence-gated handoffs with a founder-led operator.
  • A clear incident mirror you can repeat safely — non-production data only.
  • Reconnect, replay, and fencing-token behavior verified against your scenario.

What the first handoff measures

We record time from pairing to first evidence-gated handoff, whether a second handoff can happen without founder assistance, reconnect and replay behavior, unsupported host paths, and the boundary between a useful coordinator and another chat transcript.

What Finalisma does NOT claim

  • No public multi-tenant SaaS — this is a private, founder-led pilot.
  • No hosted SLA, SSO, or enterprise compliance surface.
  • No universal host compatibility — MCP-capable hosts only, verified paths documented separately.
  • No production-data readiness — non-production incident mirrors only.
